Isle of Mull Holiday Cottage

Achadh-a'Mhullaich is located on the beautiful Isle of Mull. One of many Inner Hebridean islands scattered on the western side of Scotland. Richard Hannabuss started building the stone croft cottage from an original ruin, as a young teacher back in the 1950’s over countless school holidays. His efforts spanning 3 decades, have given the Hannabuss family many happy holidays over the years.

Sleeps up to 6 people

2 double bedrooms (one ensuite).
1 Bunk room

Whole Property to Yourself

You will be renting the entire building. There is no ajoining owner or neighbours who live on site.

Dog friendly

We welcome and allow a maxiumium of two dogs for a small extra charge £25 per dog.

Plenty of room

Downstairs open plan (kitchen, dining, living room). 2 living rooms (upstairs). Utility area.

Child and baby friendly

For those wet days we have Toys, DVD's and plenty of art materials. A cot is also available.

Art/Studio facilities

Attached to the main house is a studio area, for painting and drawing.

Close to the beach

There are beautiful unspoilt quiet beaches, either a walk, or short drive away.

Facilities

Bed linen and towels provided! Free WiFi, television, DVD player, radio, CD player, coal fire, and electric heating.

The House has been rebuilt with extensive use of wood in the panelling of the walls, exposed beams and some of the furniture and fittings. It has an authentic character of its own, which some have described as a unique backwoods cabin / cottage. The layout of the house is suitable for a group of up to six people, but also it can be intimate enough for quiet holiday for two.

Isle of Mull Activities

The Isle of Mull has so much to offer, to keep you and the family busy. Spectacular walks, boat trips, wildlife tours, whale watching, quad biking, to name but a few.

Stunning Walking

Dramatic coastlines, tall mountains, forestry, rolling hills. You can discover it all on foot.

Plentiful Wildlife

Otters, Birds of Prey, Deer, Seals, Dolphins, Whales. You’re guaranteed to see a huge range of animals during your visit.

Natural Unspoilt Beaches

White sands, turquoise waters, maybe the beach to yourself. Isle of Mull delivers on some spectacular natural beaches. Although we can’t promise the water will be warm.

Gourmet Food, and High Quality Producers

The various island restaurants truly know how to showcase the islands high quality produce.
